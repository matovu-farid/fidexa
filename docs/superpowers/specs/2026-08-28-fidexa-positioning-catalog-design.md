# Fidexa Positioning and Catalog Design

Date: 2026-08-28
Status: Approved for implementation

## Intent

Position Fidexa as a software product studio that builds end-to-end products for companies, rather than as a portfolio organized around a few existing operational systems. Existing launches remain useful proof, but they must read as examples of a wider practice.

## Approved copy direction

- Hero heading: “We build software products from first idea to everyday use.”
- Hero support: “Fidexa partners with companies to turn complex operations, new ideas, and ambitious plans into thoughtful digital products.”
- Hero proof label: “Client products / Web · Mobile · Systems”.
- Selected work heading: “A few things we’ve built.”
- Selected work support: “Selected work from a much broader studio practice.”
- Studio support: “We take products from a sharp first idea to a dependable everyday experience.”
- Client Solutions title: “End-to-end product building.”
- Contact heading: “Have something worth building?”

The copy should describe the capability and relationship first, with projects presented as evidence rather than as the definition of the studio.

## Catalog decision

Retire the following obvious toy/demo or outdated portfolio entries from the public catalog:

- Space Travellers
- Stocks App
- Painter
- Virtual Phone
- Pearl of Africa Tour

Keep the serious product, client, infrastructure, and published-library work. The curated catalog therefore contains 11 projects. Do not show an empty Cross-Platform filter; the category can be reintroduced when a relevant project returns.

## Boundaries

- Keep the three real featured product captures and their order: Rishi, Money Lending, Inventory.
- Keep `/`, `/projects`, `/sms`, contact, AI support, and external product links working.
- Do not add pricing or changelog pages.
- Do not alter Penpot/Pencil boards in this implementation-only copy pass.
- Keep project descriptions substantive on `/projects`; the catalog is a sample of the practice, not a claim that it is exhaustive.

## Acceptance criteria

- Homepage copy no longer frames Fidexa as primarily an inventory/lending/spreadsheet studio.
- `/projects` excludes all five retired entries and presents five non-empty filters: All, AI & Automation, Apple & Native Apps, Web Applications, Developer Tools.
- Counts are All 11, AI & Automation 2, Apple & Native Apps 1, Web Applications 5, Developer Tools 3.
- Hero and project catalog remain legible without overflow at MacBook, iPad, and iPhone target sizes.
- Production typecheck/build pass; browser checks confirm no console errors or horizontal overflow.
